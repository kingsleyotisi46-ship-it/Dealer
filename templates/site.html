{% elif page == 'faq' %}
HELP CENTER

Questions, answered clearly.

Helpful details about using your account and submitting requests.

When is a deposit credited?

After an authorized administrator independently verifies the payment and marks the request successful. Uploading a receipt alone does not confirm payment.

Does a stock request mean shares were purchased?

No. The site records a request and does not connect to a brokerage or execute trades.

How do withdrawals work?

Submit your requested amount and bank details. The request must be reviewed; submission is not confirmation that payment has been sent.

Can fund recovery be guaranteed?

No. Recovery outcomes are uncertain. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed recovery or requesting unexpected upfront fees.

How do I check a request?

Sign in and open your dashboard or the relevant member tool to view recorded requests and statuses.
